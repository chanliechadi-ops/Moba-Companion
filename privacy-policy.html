Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MOBA Companion handles information when you use its independent game-reference features and optional promotional access-key feature.

Effective date: November 13, 2026

1. Overview

MOBA Companion is an independent reference app offering hero guides, builds, item details, spells, emblems, and visual galleries. You can access the core reference features without creating an account or logging in.

The app may offer an optional, limited-time promotional access key on eligible devices. Requesting a key is optional and is separate from the core reference features.

2. Information we collect

Core reference features: No account registration is required. The app does not ask you to submit your name, email address, contacts, or personal photos to browse its core guides and catalogs.

Optional promotional key requests: If you choose to request a free access key, the app sends the following to its server:

  • Device identifier: A one-way SHA-256-derived identifier based on the device's Android ID. The server applies additional hashing to help enforce a one-claim-per-device limit.
  • Country information: Your device's locale country code. This is not GPS or precise location data.
  • Connection metadata: Ordinary network information, such as your IP address, may be received by the server or hosting provider when a request is made.

MOBA Companion does not request GPS or precise-location permission. It does not use a dedicated analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the version described by this policy.

3. How information is used

Information sent during optional key requests is used to process the request, determine eligibility, enforce device claim limits, and help prevent abuse or fraudulent repeated claims. The app also uses network connections to retrieve reference content and images.

MOBA Companion does not currently include an advertising SDK or in-app advertising placements.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information or share optional key-request identifiers with third parties for independent advertising purposes. Hosting and network service providers may process connection and request data on our behalf to operate the app's services.

Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

5. Storage and retention

Images you choose to save from supported catalogs are downloaded to your device's local gallery. The app does not upload your personal gallery photos or videos.

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes app-managed local data subject to your device's storage behavior. Files you intentionally saved to your gallery may need to be deleted separately.

Server-side records used to enforce one-time promotional claims may remain after the app is uninstalled. A fixed server retention period has not been specified for these records.

6. Security

App requests to the described server endpoints use HTTPS encryption in transit. Device identifiers used for optional key claims are transformed using one-way hashing. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices

  • You can use the core reference features without requesting a promotional key.
  • You can avoid sending optional key-request data by not using the promotional key feature.
  • You can clear app storage or uninstall MOBA Companion to remove app-managed local data.
  • You can separately delete catalog images you saved to your device gallery.

MOBA Companion does not create user accounts. A dedicated server-data deletion-request process is not currently offered.

8. Children's privacy

MOBA Companion is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not primarily directed at children under 13. If you believe information relating to a child under 13 was submitted through the optional key-request feature, please contact us using the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the app, its services, or legal requirements. Any revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
